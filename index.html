ABOUT THE PI

Physical insight.
Climate relevance.

My work focuses on the physical processes that govern climate, from the thermodynamics of land–atmosphere exchange to the behaviour of precipitation and other extremes. I am interested in bringing together theory, observations, and modelling to better understand the Earth system.

My academic journey includes doctoral research with the Max Planck Institute for Biogeochemistry and Karlsruhe Institute of Technology, graduate study at IIT Bombay, and undergraduate study at Aligarh Muslim University.

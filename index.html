04 / ABOUT

SG·

PHYSICAL INSIGHT.
CLIMATE RELEVANCE.

Research rooted in physical understanding.

I am an Assistant Professor at the Kotak School of Sustainability, Indian Institute of Technology Kanpur. My work focuses on the physical processes that govern climate, from the thermodynamics of land–atmosphere exchange to the behaviour of precipitation and other extremes.

I received my Ph.D. through the Max Planck Institute for Biogeochemistry and Karlsruhe Institute of Technology in Germany. My academic background also includes water resources engineering at IIT Bombay and civil engineering at Aligarh Muslim University.
